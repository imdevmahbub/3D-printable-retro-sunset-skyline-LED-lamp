Print Engineering Rules
  • Max Bed Size: Every split part ≤ 158 × 196 mm (fits 220 × 220 × 250 mm bed).
  • Profile: PLA/PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, ≥1.2 mm walls (3 perimeters).
  • Joints: Concealed keyed dovetails & tongue-and-groove with 0.25 mm clearance (glue-free).
  • Support-Free: All plates print flat; overhangs ≤45°, bridges ≤14 mm.
5V USB LED & Power Budget
  • Strip: 50 cm 5V WS2812B (60 LEDs/m = 30 LEDs), 10 mm PCB width, 12 mm diffuser clearance.
  • Power: 5V USB-A/C, max ~1.2A–1.5A @ full white; typical synthwave gradient ~0.6A (3W). Use 5V/2A USB PSU.
  • Heat & Safety: Low-voltage 5V only (no mains wiring). Rear enclosure includes upper convection vents & strain-relief cable post.
Assembly Sequence (Glue-Free)
1. Join Base L/R (`01A+01B`) via keyed dovetail.
2. Join Rear Enclosure L/R (`07A+07B`); clip in 5V LED strip & route USB cable through strain relief.
3. Slide L4 Frosted Sky (`05A+05B`) & Amber Sun (`06`) into rear slots.
4. Join & seat L3 Teal (`04A+04B`), L2 Purple (`03A+03B`), and L1 Black (`02A+02B`) into base channels.
5. Snap Rear Access Cover (`08`) into place.
